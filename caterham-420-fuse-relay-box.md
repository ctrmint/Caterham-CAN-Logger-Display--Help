# Caterham 420 fuse and relay box layout

## Orientation

- **Read the table top to bottom as the box sits in the diagram.** The diagram's caption "Bottom of Fuse box" sits under its last row, so row 1 is the top of the box and row 18 is the bottom.
- **The box is six stacked groups of three fuses.** Each group has one relay beside it, on the **right-hand side** of the fuse column in both the diagram and the photo of the box under the dash.
- **Photo cross-check.** Reading the photo from top to bottom, the fuse colours match this table row for row: red, red, brown / yellow, red, blue / green, blue, blue / red, brown, blue / red, brown, red / blue, blue, tan.
- **Caveat.** The numbers printed on the fuses and relays read sideways in the photo, and I haven't confirmed which way up the box is mounted in the car. Match the fuse colour and rating to the table before relying on a position.
